George Brown College 
George Brown College is a public college located in Toronto
The college offers programs in many fields, some of them are: technology, business, health sciences and community services
George Brown College emphasizes practical learning and career preparation
Many of their programs combine classroom instruction with hands on lab activities, helping students develop skills relevant to their chosen programs 
George Brown College has several campuses in downtown Toronto those campuses are:  St. James Campus, Casa Loma Campus, and Waterfront Campus
These campuses provide learning spaces, laboratories, and facilities designed to support students in different programs
George Brown college provides student support services such as academic advising, library resources, and career guidence
These resources help students succeed academically, develop professional skills, and prepare for employment after graduation
