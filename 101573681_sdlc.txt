DevOps in the software development life cycle
DevOps plays an important role in the software development life cycle by improving collaboration between development and operations teams
DevOps practices help automate software testing, integration, and deployment, making development faster and more reliable
Continuous integration and continuous delivery allow teams to release software updates more frequently while reducing the risk of errors
DevOps also improves communication between developers, testers, and operations teams throughout the software development lifecycl
By encouraging colaboration and continuous feedback, teams can identify issues earlier, reduce delays, and maintain software more efficiently after deployment
